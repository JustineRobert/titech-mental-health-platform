# Privacy by Design

The platform uses data minimization, purpose limitation, need-to-know access, consent records, auditability, retention controls and controlled export/delete workflows.

The repository does not claim automatic legal or regulatory compliance. Applicable obligations depend on deployment jurisdiction, data controller/processor roles, contractual context and the nature of the service. Qualified privacy/legal review is a production gate.

## Sensitive-field review

Before adding any mental-health data field, document: purpose, readers, writers, retention, storage, encryption, auditing, exportability, deletion and consent requirements.
