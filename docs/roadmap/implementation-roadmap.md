# Enterprise Implementation Roadmap

The platform follows the 0–27 phased roadmap requested for TITech Mental Health Platform: discovery, engineering foundation, identity/security/tenancy, core entities, privacy/consent, assessments, scheduling, care management, safety/referrals, communications, resources, reporting, enterprise administration, files, integrations, AI assistance, accessibility/localization, performance, hardening, observability, DR, CI/CD, E2E validation, governance review, controlled pilot, production, stabilization and continuous improvement.

## Gate rule

Do not advance a phase with unresolved critical dependencies. `BLOCKED` is an acceptable and required status when evidence is missing.
